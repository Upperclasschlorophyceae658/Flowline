## 🎯 Tips for Your First Run

### Start Slow
Don't worry about speed at first. Take time to get comfortable with carving left and right using the `A` and `D` keys. Feel how the mountain responds.

### Practice Your Pop
Press `Space` or `W` to pop off the terrain. Time it right when you hit a rise or bump to get the most air.

### Learn to Flip
Once you're comfortable jumping, try pressing `Q` for a back flip or `E` for a front flip while in the air. With practice, you'll land them cleanly every time.

### Use Tuck for Speed
Press and hold `S` or `↓` to tuck and gain speed. This is especially useful when you want to catch up with friends or chase a personal best time.

### Don't Stress About Perfection
Flowline is about the experience, not perfection. Relax, enjoy the view, and let the mountain guide you.

## ❓ Troubleshooting

**Q: The game won't start. What should I do?**
First, make sure you've completed the installation process correctly. Try restarting your computer and launching again. If the problem persists, check that your graphics drivers are up to date.

**Q: The game runs slowly. How can I improve performance?**
Close other applications running in the background, especially web browsers or video streaming services. Lower your screen resolution if the game continues to lag.

**Q: Where are my game saves?**
Flowline automatically saves your progress as you play. No need to manually save - just exit the game and your settings and stats are preserved.

**Q: I can't hear any music.**
Press the `M` key to toggle music on and off. Also check that your system volume isn't muted and your speakers or headphones are properly connected.

**Q: How do I update to the latest version?**
Simply download and run the newest version from the download link above. The latest version will replace your older one.
